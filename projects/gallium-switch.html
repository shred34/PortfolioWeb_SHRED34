Gallium Switch

A heated metal funnel, a severed cable through an ice cube tray, LED panels. A cube of gallium — a metal that melts just above room temperature — is placed in the funnel. It melts, flows into the mould, bridges the two cut ends of the cable. The circuit closes, the room goes dark, the panels light up with an animation symbolising metamorphosis. Remove the solidified cube and everything resets — ready to go again. The piece is about becoming the person others want to see in pursuit of success. It ends with one question: can you shine without disguising yourself?
