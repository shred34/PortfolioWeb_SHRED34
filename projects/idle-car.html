Idle Car

Idle Car is a proof of concept for an action game with the visual grammar of old survival horror. The premise: you're sitting in a parked car, engine off, and something is happening outside. You don't move — you watch, react, and decide. The game revolves around three camera views you switch between freely: rear-view, cockpit, and dashcam. Each angle reveals different information, and where you choose to look is part of the tension. Cinematics play out around the car, choices shape the story, and the next step is simple — starting the engine. A beta is available to download for Mac and Windows (Mac: minor visual glitches).

The cockpit is where most of the game is spent — a dashboard, a centre screen relaying the dashcam, and whatever the windscreen happens to frame. Headphones matter here: much of what happens takes place outside the car, and sound usually reaches you before the image does.

Exhibited at KIKK Festival 2026 in Namur, Belgium.
