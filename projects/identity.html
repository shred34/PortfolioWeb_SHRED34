CFF Refont

A full redesign proposal for the CFF website — Switzerland's national railway. Rather than refining the existing interface, this project takes a more experimental direction: a darker aesthetic built around a line-based grid system where navigation elements recall wagons sliding along rails, with red as the only accent colour against an otherwise black and white palette. Black-and-white accelerated footage runs through the interface with dissolve effects, giving the site an almost cinematic quality. A selection of features from the original — ticketing, timetables, subscription plans — have been redesigned within the new system. The video documents a complete walkthrough of the prototype.

Pink Link

A Chrome extension that invades any web page with floating buttons — alive in their behaviour, obstructing the content beneath. The extension reads the page's metadata: specifically its internal and external links. Each link becomes a button, one-to-one — exactly as many buttons as links on the page. Pink buttons lead to a random external link, black to a random internal one. The result is a tool for drifting through the web at random, one click at a time.

Maison Jaffar

A dynamic visual identity for Maison Jaffar, a fictional photography-centred art space. The concept is built around crossroads — the intersections life forces us through. Voronoi diagrams form the backbone: their rounded, outlined cells naturally produce a network of crossings that embodies the concept. Fisheye and spherisation effects applied within the cells generate animations, accompanied by sound composed for the project. The logo exists in four adaptive variations. I also built a web tool integrating Voronoi generation, adaptive logo and text placement, photo spherisation, colour control, and more. Everything produced — business cards, T-shirts, posters, the fictional landing page — was made with it.
