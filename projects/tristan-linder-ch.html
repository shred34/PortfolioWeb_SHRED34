Tristan-Linder.ch

I designed a portfolio website for photographer Tristan Linder, using a minimalist graphic style that puts the focus on what matters most — his photographs. The site features carefully crafted micro-interactions and is fully responsive.

Visit website

I also had the opportunity to direct a video introducing Jaffar, an alter ego of Tristan Linder still in development. For this piece I combined footage shot on an old camcorder with 3D computer-generated imagery made in Blender to break the visual logic. I also composed the music myself. The video is featured on Tristan Linder's website.
