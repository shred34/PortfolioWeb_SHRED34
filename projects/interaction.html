3-2-1-0

3-2-1-0 is a set of four interconnected webtoys, each built around the theme of skincare — pushed into a territory that mixes disgust with satisfaction. The interaction is at the centre of everything — satisfying, but not always comfortable. Each digit is its own self-contained experience, and together they form a coherent countdown. The 3 is a numeral covered in razor-stubble — you shave it clean. The 2 is formed by a field of pimples, which when popped release their contents and slowly compose the shape of the digit. The 1 is a thick scab that you scratch away, gradually uncovering a raw, bleeding wound beneath. The 0 takes the shape of an ear packed with earwax, which you clean out using a cotton swab. Each stage is designed so that the interaction itself generates the visual — nothing resolves until you engage with it.

Webtoy Cam Lazer

A webtoy where you play as the cursor. Cameras track your movement and fire laser beams — get hit and your cursor disintegrates, charred, before reappearing on the right side. The native cursor is hidden and replaced with a custom PNG to make the animation work. To win, you have to unplug the cameras — their cables are visible in the bottom-left corner. Once they're all disconnected, you can smash the camera tree pole.

Mickey Incremental

A top-down shooter built in Unreal Engine 5. You play as Mickey — your only goal is to kill other Mickeys. Each kill adds one to your score. The counter never resets: even when you die and respawn, it keeps climbing. There is no end state, only accumulation. A small game about identity and repetition.

Dad's Trauma House

A branching 3D animation set inside a house. Across four rooms, you trace a life path — one of several possible — rooted in a childhood with an alcoholic and violent father. The choices you make in relation to him shape how your life unfolds as you move from room to room. Not a happy story. It is told through 3D environments, sound that intensifies the further you sink, and colour used deliberately as an expressive tool — contrast shifting with the weight of what unfolds.

Hyper

A 3D interactive animation with branching choices. Set in a future where AI has seized control of the planet and decimated the human species, you play as a drone deployed on Earth, tasked by a central server with locating and eliminating the last surviving human. The piece is built around a full storyline, unfolding through a constant back-and-forth with the server. When you finally find the human, two endings await: kill, or spare.

Memoryshell

A small project for visualising personal memories. An egg floats at the centre of a rotating 3D universe. A set of buttons with strange names — echoing old video game saves or forgotten file names — each correspond to a memory. Clicking one snaps you suddenly closer to the egg, and through its surface you see the memory play, accompanied by sound treated with reverb and echo.
