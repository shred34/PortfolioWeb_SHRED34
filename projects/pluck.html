Pluck Sountoy

Pluck is an interactive sound toy built in JavaScript. The screen is filled with circles grouped into clusters — clicking a circle launches it toward another group, triggering a note and a chain reaction. Each circle has its own pitch based on its size, and clusters are organized by octave, from low to high. The game loops infinitely. The goal, if there is one, is to reduce the number of circles on screen as much as possible — a live count displayed in the background keeps track.

Visit website

The game opens on a title screen made entirely of circles arranged to form the word Pluck. It is playable on all platforms — optimised for iPad and desktop, where the circles are large and comfortable to interact with, though it works just as well on iPhone, even if the smaller circles make things a little harder.
