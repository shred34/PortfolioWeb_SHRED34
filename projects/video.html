Systematic Noise

A two-minute video sequence made in collaboration with Barthélémy Anhorn, a geophysicist. The starting point was his master's thesis at ETH Zurich, in which he was tasked with diagnosing a fault in a specialised georadar — an instrument suspended beneath a helicopter that sweeps across glaciers to build detailed cartographic data. The radar was sensitive to a specific interference, and his mission was to identify its source. I began by recording him speaking about this work in a monologue, then built the visuals around his words: a combination of CGI sequences modelled in Blender and archival footage from an old documentary. The audio was mixed and refined to support the rhythm of the piece — voice, music and image working together into a single cohesive sequence.
