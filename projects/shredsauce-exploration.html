Shredsauce Exploration

Here I have brought together the full body of work I created around Shredsauce, a very particular winter sports game that offers an almost infinite creative freedom—full customization of look, grabs, and levels, all shared through a community cloud. The community itself stays fairly niche but present, with a real culture of creation and sharing behind it. At the core of this project is a graphic edition, Shredsauce Compound System, which documents and demystifies this creation system that has never had any official documentation despite years of existence. The edition breaks down the game's mechanics—the creation and assignment of grabs, gear, and levels—before showing how these tools translate into actual skiing practice, and closes with more personal visual experiments born from the game's margins and bugs.

The project also includes an experimental website and an animated poster, both extensions of the same exploration. The site is a visual representation of the dopamine Shredsauce produces—characters from the game, caught mid-motion and more or less glitched, layered on top of each other through scrollytelling to build an increasingly dense accumulation of images, a sensory translation of what it feels like to be pulled deeper into the system scroll after scroll. The animated poster carries the same logic into a fixed, standalone form—a composition of fragments from the game set in motion, built as an independent piece rather than a linear narrative.
