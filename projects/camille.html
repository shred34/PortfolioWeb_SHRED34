Camille

CAMILLE is a wall-mounted personal wellness assistant that invites users to reflect by asking them questions at different times of the day. Each prompt is a small moment of pause — an opportunity to check in with oneself amid the noise of daily life. The device's index function allows users to review their past week or month, taking time to pause and reflect, and gradually building a personal record of thoughts and feelings over time.

Project created in collaboration with Timofei Cruz.

The device itself is screenless and text-free — interaction happens through two buttons, a slider, and a central button, with responses communicated entirely through sound and light.
